About

"Her essays are humorous by instinct, but they don't look away from what's underneath."

Mimi writes personal essays and creative nonfiction shaped by growing up between two cultures — the daughter of immigrants, then a mother herself, watching the same questions of belonging show up in a new generation. Her work takes on race, identity, and culture, and the news of the day as it lands on an ordinary household.

She lives in the Washington, DC area, where she's a wife, a mother, and a yoga teacher — and, more recently, an aspiring guitar player, much to her family's patient amusement.
